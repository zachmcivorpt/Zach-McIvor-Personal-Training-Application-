# APEX Coaching Platform — Data Processing & Privacy Terms

**Effective Date:** 6 September 2026
**Last Updated:** 6 September 2026

## 1. Purpose

These Data Processing & Privacy Terms ("DPT") govern the processing of Client Data by APEX Coaching Platform ("APEX") where a coach, coaching business or organisation uses APEX to collect, store, manage or otherwise process information relating to clients.

These DPT form part of the agreement between APEX and the relevant coach or organisation and should be read with the APEX Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Coach Responsibility and Client Data Agreement.

## 2. Definitions

**"Client"**
An individual receiving coaching or related services from a Coach.

**"Client Data"**
Any information about a Client that is uploaded, entered, transmitted, generated or otherwise processed through APEX on behalf of a Coach or Organisation.
Client Data may include personal information and, depending on what is collected, sensitive information or health information.

**"Coach"**
A person or entity using APEX to provide coaching or related services.

**"Organisation"**
A business or other entity that uses APEX and controls Client Data.

**"Processing"**
Any operation performed on information, including collection, storage, access, retrieval, transmission, organisation, modification, deletion or disclosure.

## 3. Roles of the parties

For Client Data uploaded by a Coach or Organisation:

* the Coach or Organisation is primarily responsible for determining the purposes for which Client Data is collected and used;
* APEX processes Client Data to provide, maintain, secure and support the Platform; and
* APEX will not independently determine the substantive coaching purpose for which Client Data is collected.

The terminology of "controller" and "processor" may be used as convenient descriptions of the parties' functions, but these DPT do not create obligations under a foreign data protection law unless that law separately applies.

## 4. Instructions

APEX will process Client Data in accordance with:

* the Coach's or Organisation's lawful instructions;
* the applicable service agreement;
* these DPT;
* the APEX Terms of Service; and
* applicable Australian privacy laws.

APEX may process information without separate instructions where reasonably necessary to:

* provide the Platform;
* maintain security;
* prevent fraud;
* troubleshoot;
* maintain backups;
* comply with law;
* respond to legal process;
* investigate misuse; or
* protect APEX, its users or third parties.

## 5. Coach responsibilities

The Coach or Organisation must ensure that Client Data is collected and used lawfully.

This includes responsibility for:

* identifying the relevant purpose of collection;
* providing appropriate privacy notices;
* obtaining consent where legally required;
* obtaining appropriate authority to upload information to APEX;
* ensuring sensitive information is collected appropriately;
* avoiding unnecessary collection;
* maintaining data quality;
* responding to Client access or correction requests where appropriate;
* establishing appropriate retention periods; and
* ensuring that their own practices comply with applicable laws.

## 6. Sensitive and health information

Client Data may include health information.

Health information may constitute "sensitive information" under the Privacy Act.

A Coach must not collect health information merely because it may be useful.

The Coach should only collect information reasonably necessary for the service and should obtain any consent required by applicable law.

Where a Coach is itself an APP entity or otherwise subject to privacy legislation, the Coach remains responsible for meeting its obligations concerning sensitive information.

APEX will provide reasonable Platform functionality to support appropriate handling of such information but cannot determine whether a Coach's collection is lawful.

## 7. Security obligations

APEX will maintain reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect Client Data against:

* misuse;
* interference;
* loss;
* unauthorised access;
* unauthorised modification; and
* unauthorised disclosure.

Measures may include:

* authentication;
* access controls;
* encryption where appropriate;
* logging and monitoring;
* infrastructure security;
* backup controls;
* incident response;
* staff confidentiality obligations; and
* vendor security assessment.

## 8. Coach security obligations

The Coach must:

* protect account credentials;
* use strong passwords;
* enable multi-factor authentication where available;
* restrict access to authorised personnel;
* remove access when personnel cease requiring it;
* not download Client Data unnecessarily;
* not share Client Data through insecure channels where avoidable;
* promptly report suspected breaches; and
* maintain appropriate security on devices used to access APEX.

## 9. Subprocessors and third-party providers

APEX may engage third-party service providers to support the Platform.

These may include providers of:

* cloud infrastructure;
* data storage;
* communications;
* authentication;
* payments;
* analytics;
* customer support;
* security;
* monitoring;
* backup;
* software development; and
* other infrastructure.

APEX remains responsible for managing such providers to the extent required by applicable law and contractual arrangements.

## 10. Overseas processing

Client Data may be stored or processed outside Australia.

APEX will take reasonable steps to comply with applicable requirements concerning overseas disclosure, including APP 8 where applicable.

The Coach acknowledges that use of APEX may involve international infrastructure and service providers.

Where reasonably practicable, APEX will maintain information about material overseas processing locations and categories of recipients.

## 11. Disclosure required by law

APEX may disclose Client Data where reasonably necessary to:

* comply with law;
* comply with a court or tribunal order;
* respond to lawful regulatory requests;
* protect the rights, safety or property of APEX or another person;
* investigate suspected fraud or unlawful conduct; or
* address a serious security incident.

Where legally permitted, APEX will seek to provide appropriate notice before making a compelled disclosure.

## 12. Data breaches

APEX will maintain procedures for detecting, containing, assessing and responding to security incidents.

Where APEX becomes aware of a suspected breach affecting Client Data, APEX will:

1. take reasonable steps to contain the incident;
2. investigate the nature and scope of the incident;
3. assess potential harm;
4. determine whether notification is required;
5. take reasonable remediation measures; and
6. communicate with affected parties where required or appropriate.

Where the Privacy Act's Notifiable Data Breaches scheme applies, APEX will comply with applicable notification requirements.

## 13. Coach notification obligations

The Coach must promptly notify APEX if they become aware of:

* unauthorised access to their APEX account;
* accidental disclosure of Client Data;
* loss or theft of a device containing Client Data;
* suspicious account activity;
* unauthorised downloading of Client Data; or
* another incident reasonably likely to affect the security of Client Data.

## 14. Assistance

Where reasonably necessary and proportionate, APEX may assist a Coach or Organisation with:

* access requests;
* correction requests;
* deletion requests;
* data exports;
* security incidents;
* privacy complaints;
* regulatory enquiries; and
* other privacy compliance matters.

APEX may charge reasonable fees for extraordinary assistance where permitted under the applicable commercial agreement.

## 15. Data access

APEX will provide Coaches or Organisations with access to Client Data through the Platform according to their account permissions and subscription.

APEX personnel will only access Client Data where reasonably necessary for legitimate business purposes such as:

* technical support;
* security;
* troubleshooting;
* maintenance;
* legal compliance; or
* service administration.

## 16. Data retention

APEX will retain Client Data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Platform or satisfy legal, security, contractual, accounting or dispute-resolution requirements.

The Coach is responsible for determining the appropriate retention period for Client Data that it controls.

APEX may delete or de-identify Client Data following account termination, subject to:

* applicable legal obligations;
* backup cycles;
* security requirements;
* dispute preservation requirements; and
* contractual requirements.

## 17. Data export

Where technically available, APEX may allow Coaches or Organisations to export Client Data.

Exports remain subject to the Coach's privacy, confidentiality and security obligations.

Once data has been exported from APEX, the Coach is responsible for protecting that exported copy.

## 18. Data minimisation

Coaches should only collect Client Data that is reasonably necessary for the services being provided.

APEX may impose technical restrictions or warnings where it identifies unusually sensitive or excessive data collection, but APEX does not monitor every item of Client Data for necessity.

## 19. Data quality

The Coach is responsible for ensuring that information entered by the Coach is accurate and appropriately maintained.

APEX is not responsible for verifying the factual accuracy of Client Data.

## 20. Use of aggregated and de-identified data

APEX may create aggregated or de-identified information from Platform data where reasonably necessary for:

* analytics;
* security;
* product development;
* performance monitoring;
* service improvement; or
* business reporting.

APEX will take reasonable steps to prevent such information from identifying individuals where it is represented as de-identified.

APEX will not intentionally use identifiable Client Data for unrelated commercial purposes merely because it has access to that information.

## 21. Confidentiality

Each party must protect confidential information received from the other party.

APEX personnel and contractors with access to Client Data will be subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

## 22. Privacy requests from Clients

Where a Client asks APEX for access to information that is controlled by a Coach or Organisation, APEX may direct the Client to the relevant Coach or Organisation.

APEX will provide reasonable assistance where required.

Where APEX independently holds the information as the relevant APP entity, APEX will handle the request in accordance with applicable privacy law.

## 23. Regulatory cooperation

The parties will reasonably cooperate with each other in responding to lawful privacy regulator enquiries relating to the Platform.

Nothing requires either party to disclose information where disclosure would breach applicable law, privilege or confidentiality obligations.

## 24. Audit and compliance information

APEX may provide reasonable information concerning its security and privacy practices where commercially appropriate.

Any security documentation provided by APEX is confidential and must not be distributed publicly without permission.

## 25. International requirements

Where the Coach or Organisation requires compliance with privacy legislation outside Australia, the parties should enter into additional terms addressing those requirements before processing begins.

These DPT do not automatically represent compliance with every international privacy regime.

## 26. Conflict

If these DPT conflict with the APEX Terms of Service regarding the processing of Client Data, these DPT will prevail to the extent of the conflict.

## 27. Survival

Privacy, confidentiality, intellectual property, security, liability and data protection obligations that are intended to survive termination will continue after termination to the extent necessary.
