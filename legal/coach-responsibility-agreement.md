# APEX Coaching Platform — Coach Responsibility & Client Data Agreement

**Effective Date:** 6 September 2026
**Last Updated:** 6 September 2026

## 1. Purpose

This Coach Responsibility & Client Data Agreement ("Agreement") establishes the responsibilities of coaches and coaching businesses using APEX Coaching Platform ("APEX") in relation to their clients, professional services, client information, privacy and use of the Platform.

This Agreement is intended to operate alongside the APEX Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Data Processing & Privacy Terms.

## 2. Independent coach responsibility

The Coach acknowledges that APEX is a technology platform and not the provider of the Coach's professional services.

The Coach is solely responsible for:

* their relationship with each Client;
* services they provide;
* advice they give;
* programs they create;
* information they enter into APEX;
* representations they make to Clients;
* professional standards applicable to their services;
* qualifications and registrations they claim to hold;
* insurance requirements;
* client consent and onboarding processes; and
* compliance with laws applicable to their professional activities.

APEX does not supervise, approve or endorse a Coach's professional advice.

## 3. APEX does not provide coaching

The Coach acknowledges that APEX itself does not provide:

* fitness coaching;
* personal training;
* medical advice;
* healthcare;
* physiotherapy;
* rehabilitation;
* psychological services;
* dietetic services;
* nutrition coaching; or
* other professional health services.

Any such service provided through APEX is provided by the Coach or relevant independent professional.

The Coach must not represent to a Client that APEX has assessed, approved or guaranteed the Coach's professional advice.

## 4. Professional qualifications

The Coach must only represent qualifications, registrations, memberships, certifications or professional experience that they genuinely hold.

The Coach must not use APEX to provide a regulated professional service unless they are legally authorised and appropriately qualified to do so.

The Coach is responsible for maintaining any registration, certification, insurance or continuing professional development required for their services.

## 5. Medical boundaries

A Coach must not use APEX to represent themselves as a medical practitioner or healthcare provider unless they are appropriately qualified and authorised.

Where a Client presents with symptoms, injury, illness or other circumstances outside the Coach's competence, the Coach should recommend that the Client seek appropriate professional advice.

The Coach must not use APEX as a substitute for appropriate medical assessment.

## 6. Fitness and physical activity

A Coach providing fitness or exercise-related services is responsible for assessing whether those services are appropriate within their professional scope.

APEX does not assess:

* exercise suitability;
* physical capacity;
* injury risk;
* medical risk;
* technique;
* environmental hazards; or
* emergency situations.

The Coach remains responsible for appropriate professional processes.

## 7. Nutrition

The Coach must not represent general nutritional information as medical or dietetic advice unless appropriately qualified and authorised.

Where nutrition or dietary services are provided, the Coach is responsible for ensuring that those services fall within their legal and professional scope.

APEX does not independently verify nutrition advice entered into the Platform.

## 8. Client consent

The Coach must obtain any consent required by law before collecting, using or disclosing Client Data.

Consent processes should be:

* appropriately informed;
* voluntary where required;
* sufficiently specific;
* recorded where appropriate; and
* capable of being withdrawn where applicable.

Consent must not be treated as a substitute for another legal requirement where the law requires a different basis for handling information.

## 9. Privacy notice

The Coach must provide Clients with appropriate information about:

* what Client Data is collected;
* why it is collected;
* how it is used;
* who it may be disclosed to;
* whether it may be processed overseas;
* how Clients can access or correct their information; and
* how privacy complaints can be made.

Where the Coach is an APP entity, the Coach must maintain an appropriate APP Privacy Policy.

## 10. Collection of Client Data

The Coach must only collect Client Data that is reasonably necessary for their services.

The Coach should not request sensitive information merely because APEX makes it possible to collect such information.

The Coach must consider whether each category of information is:

* necessary;
* relevant;
* proportionate;
* lawful to collect; and
* appropriately protected.

## 11. Health and sensitive information

The Coach acknowledges that information concerning health may constitute sensitive information under Australian privacy law.

The Coach must comply with applicable requirements for collecting sensitive information.

Where consent is required, the Coach must obtain appropriate consent before collecting the information.

The Coach should avoid entering unnecessary medical information into free-text fields.

## 12. Client photographs and videos

If a Coach uploads photographs or videos of a Client, the Coach is responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary authority and consent.

The Coach must consider:

* the purpose of the image;
* whether the image is reasonably necessary;
* whether the Client has been informed of its use;
* whether the image contains sensitive information; and
* how long the image should be retained.

## 13. Client communications

Coaches must use professional and appropriate communication practices when communicating with Clients through APEX.

The Coach must not use APEX communications to:

* harass;
* threaten;
* discriminate;
* sexually harass;
* deceive;
* exploit;
* pressure; or
* otherwise abuse a Client.

## 14. Client confidentiality

The Coach must protect Client information and only disclose it where authorised or legally permitted.

Client Data must not be:

* sold;
* publicly disclosed;
* posted to social media;
* shared with unrelated businesses; or
* provided to third parties

unless the disclosure is authorised, reasonably necessary for the service or otherwise permitted or required by law.

## 15. Third-party services

The Coach acknowledges that APEX may rely upon third-party technology providers.

The Coach must not upload Client Data to an external service through an APEX integration unless the Coach has considered whether the disclosure is lawful and appropriate.

Where a third-party integration is enabled by the Coach, the Coach is responsible for reviewing that service's relevant privacy and security arrangements.

## 16. Overseas disclosures

The Coach acknowledges that Client Data processed through APEX may be handled by infrastructure or service providers located outside Australia.

Where the Coach is subject to the Privacy Act, the Coach remains responsible for complying with applicable requirements concerning overseas disclosure.

APEX will address its own obligations concerning overseas recipients under applicable privacy law.

## 17. Client access and correction

Clients may have rights to access and correct personal information.

The Coach must maintain a reasonable process for responding to Client requests.

Where Client Data is controlled by the Coach, APEX may direct the Client to the Coach.

The Coach must not unreasonably obstruct a lawful access or correction request.

## 18. Client deletion requests

Where a Client requests deletion of information, the Coach must consider the request under applicable law.

Information may need to be retained where:

* required by law;
* necessary for legal claims;
* required for professional records;
* necessary to resolve a dispute; or
* otherwise permitted by law.

## 19. Data retention

The Coach is responsible for establishing an appropriate retention period for Client Data.

The Coach should not retain personal information indefinitely merely because APEX provides storage capacity.

At the end of the relevant retention period, the Coach should securely delete or de-identify information where required.

## 20. Account security

The Coach must maintain appropriate security over their APEX account.

The Coach must:

* maintain strong credentials;
* enable multi-factor authentication where available;
* restrict employee access;
* remove former staff;
* not share login credentials;
* secure devices used to access Client Data; and
* promptly report suspected compromise.

## 21. Staff and contractors

If the Coach permits employees or contractors to access Client Data, the Coach must ensure those persons:

* have a legitimate need for access;
* understand their confidentiality obligations;
* receive appropriate privacy/security instructions; and
* have their access removed when no longer required.

## 22. Data breach responsibility

The Coach must promptly notify APEX of any suspected incident involving:

* unauthorised access;
* unauthorised disclosure;
* stolen credentials;
* accidental sharing;
* lost devices;
* compromised integrations; or
* Client Data being accessed by an unauthorised person.

The Coach must cooperate with reasonable incident-response requests from APEX.

Where the Coach is subject to the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme, the Coach remains responsible for determining and meeting its own notification obligations.

## 23. Client data accuracy

The Coach is responsible for the accuracy of Client Data entered by the Coach.

The Coach should correct inaccurate or outdated information when identified.

APEX does not independently verify the factual accuracy of Client Data.

## 24. Intellectual property

The Coach retains ownership of original materials they lawfully create and upload, subject to any separate agreement.

The Coach grants APEX the limited rights necessary to host, transmit, display and process those materials for the operation of the Platform.

The Coach must not upload material that infringes another person's intellectual property rights.

## 25. Client intellectual property

The Coach must respect intellectual property rights belonging to Clients or third parties.

A Coach must not upload copyrighted, confidential or proprietary third-party material unless appropriately authorised.

## 26. Acceptable use

The Coach must not use APEX to:

* conduct unlawful activity;
* collect information without appropriate authority;
* distribute malware;
* compromise another person's account;
* engage in harassment;
* make fraudulent claims;
* provide services outside their professional scope;
* knowingly provide dangerous or unlawful advice;
* misuse Client Data;
* sell Client Data;
* circumvent Platform security; or
* otherwise materially breach the APEX Terms of Service.

## 27. Records

The Coach should maintain appropriate records concerning:

* client consent;
* privacy notices;
* professional qualifications;
* relevant insurance;
* client instructions;
* material incidents;
* complaints; and
* data access or correction requests.

APEX does not act as the Coach's legal or professional record-keeping adviser.

## 28. Client safety

The Coach acknowledges that APEX cannot monitor Client safety.

The Coach remains responsible for appropriate professional risk management, including escalation to emergency or medical services where appropriate.

APEX must not be represented as an emergency service.

## 29. No guarantee of professional outcomes

The Coach must not represent that APEX guarantees:

* fitness outcomes;
* weight loss;
* medical outcomes;
* nutritional outcomes;
* injury recovery;
* performance outcomes; or
* any other professional result.

## 30. Indemnity by Coach

To the extent permitted by law, the Coach indemnifies APEX against claims, losses, liabilities and reasonable costs arising from:

* the Coach's professional services;
* the Coach's breach of privacy obligations;
* unlawful collection or disclosure of Client Data;
* infringement of third-party rights by Coach content;
* misleading representations by the Coach;
* the Coach's breach of professional obligations; or
* the Coach's unlawful use of APEX.

This indemnity does not apply to the extent the relevant loss was caused by APEX's negligence, wilful misconduct or breach of applicable law.

## 31. APEX's role and limitations

The Coach acknowledges that APEX:

* provides technology rather than professional coaching;
* does not independently verify Client Data;
* does not assess professional advice;
* does not assess Client health or fitness;
* does not guarantee coaching outcomes;
* does not replace professional judgement; and
* cannot guarantee that the Platform will be free from technical failures.

## 32. Suspension of Coach access

APEX may suspend or restrict a Coach's access where reasonably necessary to:

* protect Client Data;
* respond to a security incident;
* investigate suspected unlawful conduct;
* protect Clients;
* address a serious breach of these terms;
* comply with law; or
* protect the Platform.

Where reasonably practicable, APEX will provide notice.

## 33. Termination

APEX may terminate the Coach's access where the Coach:

* materially breaches this Agreement;
* repeatedly breaches privacy obligations;
* misuses Client Data;
* creates a material safety or security risk;
* engages in unlawful conduct; or
* ceases to be eligible to use the Platform.

Termination does not remove obligations concerning confidentiality, Client Data, intellectual property, privacy, security or liability that are intended to survive.

## 34. Australian Privacy Act

Where applicable, the Coach acknowledges their obligations under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

The Coach should have regard to obligations concerning:

* open and transparent management;
* collection;
* notification;
* use and disclosure;
* direct marketing;
* overseas disclosure;
* security;
* access; and
* correction.

The Coach acknowledges that the Privacy Act may apply differently depending on the Coach's business structure and activities.

Nothing in this Agreement represents legal advice concerning whether the Coach is an APP entity.

## 35. Changes to law

The Coach acknowledges that Australian privacy law may change.

APEX may update this Agreement, Platform functionality or privacy documentation where reasonably necessary to respond to changes in law.

## 36. Acknowledgement

By using APEX to store or process Client Data, the Coach confirms that they:

* have read this Agreement;
* understand their responsibilities;
* will only upload Client Data they are authorised to provide;
* will comply with applicable privacy obligations;
* will protect Client information;
* will maintain appropriate account security; and
* understand that APEX is a technology platform rather than a provider of fitness, medical or nutrition coaching.
