0. DISTRIBUTION — MAKE IT UNLISTED (PRIVATE LINK ONLY)
===========================================================================

You wanted clients-only access, not a public/searchable App Store
listing. Apple has a built-in option for exactly this: an "Unlisted"
app. It still goes through full App Store Review and gets a real
listing page, but it's excluded from search and browse — the only way
anyone finds it is a direct link you send them (text, email, QR code).
No TestFlight downsides either (no 90-day build expiry, no tester cap,
no re-inviting) — it behaves like a normal permanent App Store app,
just hidden from public discovery.

To set it: in App Store Connect, on the app's Pricing and Availability
page, choose "Unlisted" under App Distribution Method (instead of the
default "Public Presence"). Once approved, App Store Connect gives you
a private App Store link — that's what you send to clients.

3. DESCRIPTION (4000 char max)
===========================================================================

APEX Coaching Platform is the all-in-one training and nutrition app
built for personal trainers and their clients — one app, two views,
everything a real coaching relationship actually needs.

FOR COACHES
Run your entire roster from your phone. Build training programs with
full sets/reps/RIR prescriptions, supersets and circuits, then assign
them to clients across structured training phases. Track every
client's check-ins, progress photos, body measurements and workout
history in one place. Message clients directly, review their weekly
check-in forms, and get an automatic Recent Activity feed so nothing
falls through the cracks. Build a reusable library of workouts, meal
templates, foods and habits once — assign it to any client, forever.

FOR CLIENTS
Follow your training program day by day, log every set with weight
and notes, and see your progress tracked automatically over time —
strength history, consistency streaks, personal bests and body
metrics, all graphed. Track nutrition against coach-set targets with
a 400+ item food database, barcode scanning, and Quick Add for
logging anything by name and calories in seconds. Complete weekly
check-ins your coach actually sees. Message your coach directly, get
push notifications for new programs and replies, and never lose track
of what's due.

KEY FEATURES
- Full training program builder: phases, supersets, circuits, AMRAP,
  reps-or-time prescriptions
- Nutrition tracking: barcode scanning, Quick Add, custom meals,
  400+ food database, macro targets
- Progress tracking: strength history, body measurements, progress
  photos, personal bests, consistency streaks
- Custom check-in forms, scheduled weekly with push reminders
- Direct coach-client messaging with photo/video/PDF attachments
- Daily habit tracking and challenges
- Full training calendar with drag-and-drop scheduling
- Built for real coaching businesses — not a generic fitness tracker

Whether you're a coach managing a full client roster or a client
following your program, APEX keeps training, nutrition and
communication in one place.

5. AGE RATING QUESTIONNAIRE
===========================================================================

Apple's age rating form is a set of yes/no questions about content —
answer based on what the app actually contains, not aspirationally.
For APEX as built:

- Violence, sexual content, profanity, gambling, alcohol/tobacco/drug
  references: No, to all of them — the app has none of this.
- Horror/fear themes: No.
- Medical/treatment information: Depends on how you read it — the app
  handles fitness/nutrition data (workouts, calories, body metrics)
  but isn't itself a medical device or diagnostic tool, and the
  Privacy Policy already says APEX doesn't provide medical or health
  advice itself. Answering "No" here is defensible; if unsure, "Yes"
  just adds a content descriptor, it doesn't block approval.
- Unrestricted web access: No — the native app only loads the APEX
  domain; it doesn't act as a general browser.
- User-generated content / messaging: The app has private 1:1
  messaging between a coach and their own clients only (not public,
  not with strangers, no open chat rooms) — this typically doesn't
  trigger the stricter "unmoderated UGC" rating tier the way an open
  social app would, but confirm this in the questionnaire yourself
  since Apple's exact wording/logic can shift between versions.

Expected result: 4+ rating.

Apple treats it as your legal declaration — the draft below is a
starting point based on what's actually implemented (Firebase Auth +

substitute for you confirming it's accurate before submitting.

Data types collected (recommend declaring):
  - Contact Info: Name, Email Address
  - Health & Fitness: Fitness (workouts, activity), Health
    (body metrics, nutrition data)
  - User Content: Photos or Videos (progress photos, message
    attachments), Other User Content (messages, notes, check-in forms)
  - Identifiers: User ID
  - Usage Data: Product Interaction (if you're comfortable declaring
    general in-app activity — optional if you don't track this
    explicitly beyond what Firebase collects by default)
  - Diagnostics: Crash Data (Firebase's SDKs collect some of this by
    default even without an Analytics package explicitly added —
    worth declaring to be safe)

Data use: "App Functionality" for all of the above (this app doesn't
use data for third-party advertising or tracking).

Data linked to identity: Yes — all of the above is tied to the
signed-in user's account.

Tracking (the separate Apple "used to track you across apps/sites for
ads" question): No — nothing in this app does that.
