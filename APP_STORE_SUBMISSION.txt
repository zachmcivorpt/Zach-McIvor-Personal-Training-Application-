APEX Coaching Platform — App Store Submission Guide (iOS, on your Mac)
========================================================================

WHAT THIS IS
------------
Step-by-step instructions for packaging APEX as a native iOS app and
submitting it to the Apple App Store. This has to be done on a Mac —
Apple requires the final build to be compiled and signed with Xcode,
which only runs on macOS. Nothing here can be done from a Claude Code
cloud session; these are manual steps for you to follow yourself.

Your live app URL:      https://zach-mc-ivor-personal-training-appl.vercel.app
Your privacy policy URL: https://zach-mc-ivor-personal-training-appl.vercel.app/legal/privacy-policy


STEP 1 — Enroll in the Apple Developer Program
------------------------------------------------
1. Go to https://developer.apple.com/programs
2. Click "Enroll", sign in with your Apple ID.
3. Choose "Individual" (not "Organization" — that requires a D-U-N-S
   business number and a lot more paperwork; Individual is fine for
   publishing solo).
4. Pay the $99/year fee.
5. Approval usually takes a few hours to a day.


STEP 2 — Generate the iOS package with PWABuilder
----------------------------------------------------
1. On your Mac, open pwabuilder.com in Safari or Chrome.
2. Paste in your live app URL (above) and click "Start".
3. It scans the app and shows a readiness report — the manifest and
   icons are already set up correctly, so this should score well.
4. Click "Package for Stores" -> choose "iOS".
5. Fill in the fields it asks for:
   - Bundle ID: something like com.apexcoaching.app
   - Apple Developer Team ID: found at developer.apple.com under
     "Membership" once Step 1 is approved
6. Download the generated .zip file.


STEP 3 — Open it in Xcode
----------------------------
1. Unzip the download.
2. Open the .xcodeproj (or .xcworkspace) file inside — this launches
   Xcode automatically.
3. In Xcode's left sidebar, click the project name at the very top.
4. Go to the "Signing & Capabilities" tab.
5. Under "Team", select your Apple Developer account. Xcode will
   auto-generate the signing certificate for you.


STEP 4 — Test it
-------------------
1. At the top of the Xcode window, pick a simulator (e.g. "iPhone 15")
   from the device dropdown.
2. Click the Run button (the triangle/play icon).
3. Confirm the app opens and loads your real app content — try
   signing in, check the login screen looks right.


STEP 5 — Create the App Store listing
-----------------------------------------
1. Go to https://appstoreconnect.apple.com
2. My Apps -> the "+" button -> New App.
3. Fill in:
   - Name: APEX Coaching Platform
   - Category: Health & Fitness
   - Privacy Policy URL: (see top of this file)
4. You'll need a few screenshots — take them from the Xcode simulator
   (Simulator app -> File -> Save Screen, or Cmd+S while it's focused).
5. Write a short app description. Ask your Claude Code session to
   draft this for you if you want a hand with the wording.
6. Under "App Privacy", fill in the data-collection questionnaire —
   this should match what's disclosed in the Privacy Policy (health
   and fitness data, account info, messages/photos between coach and
   client).


STEP 6 — Archive and submit
-------------------------------
1. Back in Xcode: Product menu -> Archive. This packages the final
   release build (takes a minute or two).
2. When the Organizer window opens showing your archive, click
   "Distribute App".
3. Choose "App Store Connect" -> follow the prompts -> Upload.
4. Back in App Store Connect, go to your app listing, attach the
   build you just uploaded (may take 10-30 min to finish processing
   on Apple's end before it's selectable).
5. Click "Submit for Review".

Apple's review typically takes 1-3 days. You'll get an email either
approving it or asking for changes.


ANDROID (BONUS — doesn't need a Mac at all)
-----------------------------------------------
Same starting point, done entirely in your browser:
1. pwabuilder.com -> paste your URL -> "Package for Stores" -> Android.
2. It builds a signed .aab package for you in the cloud — no Android
   Studio needed.
3. Download it and upload directly to Google Play Console
   (play.google.com/console), $25 one-time developer fee.
4. Fill in the same kind of store listing (name, description,
   screenshots, privacy policy URL, data safety questionnaire).
