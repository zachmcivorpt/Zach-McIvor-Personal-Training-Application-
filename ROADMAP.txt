STEP 2 — MONETISATION: TRAIN SOLO FREE / PLUS / ALL ACCESS (iOS 1.0.4)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
APPROVED 2026-10-28. BUILT 2026-10-28 (web, server, rules, iOS bridge), all
switched OFF until launch: nothing changes for customers until the owner
turns it on (coach console > Train Solo > Subscriptions > Go live).

Why 1.0.4: buying inside the iPhone app needs new native code (RevenueCat's
SDK), and the subscriptions must be approved by Apple together with an app
version. 1.0.3 (Apple Health, voice notes, form checks, lock-screen rest
alert, offline) is released first; 1.0.4 is the launch release with
subscriptions and the instant-open fix (the app opens
straight from the copy saved on the phone; new releases apply on the next
open).

The plan
  FREE        create sessions and log workouts; up to 3 saved sessions;
              exercise library, swaps, calendar, history, weigh-ins,
              photos, body metrics, consistency, achievements.
  PLUS        A$9.99/month or A$79.99/year (10-day free trial on yearly).
              + suggested weights every set, build your own multi-week
              programs, full stats (strength PRs, weekly volume, performance
              timeline), Apple Health on Progress, recovery add-ons,
              unlimited saved sessions.
  ALL ACCESS  A$24.99/month or A$179.99/year (10-day free trial on yearly).
              + every program in the catalogue, and every new one.
  One free trial per customer. Programs bought before launch stay theirs on
  any tier. Single programs stop being sold the day it goes live (the server
  refuses them once live).

Failed payments (owner rule: no paid access without payment)
  - Website (Stripe): a failed renewal makes the subscription past_due and
    the customer drops to Free straight away. Stripe keeps retrying and
    emailing; if it goes through, access comes back by itself.
  - iPhone (Apple): Billing Grace Period OFF, so access ends when the
    renewal fails; Apple retries for up to 60 days.
  - Logs, sessions, built programs and history are never deleted; locked
    things come back when they pay again.
  - Enforced on the server, not just the app: programs a customer hasn't
    bought can only be read while the server-written entitlement says All
    Access is paid up (firestore.rules hasAllAccess()).

How All Access programs sit on the calendar (src/solo/enrolments.js)
  - Bought programs: exactly as before — each has its own start date and
    stays on the calendar whatever the tier. No tag.
  - All Access: ONE active program at a time, tagged "All Access" in My
    Training. "Start program" asks for a start date. Starting a different
    one pauses the current one at the week it's in (the app says so first):
    its sessions stay on the calendar up to that day as history, nothing
    after. Tapping a paused program resumes it at that same week, and the
    other one pauses.
  - The customer's own built programs (Plus) are tagged "Yours" and run
    alongside, like bought programs.
  - Subscription lapses: All Access programs (and own programs, for Plus)
    lock — off the calendar, shown "Included with All Access", history kept.
    Renewing unlocks them where they were.

The questionnaire and suggested programs
  - Unchanged matching (src/solo/recommendations.js): exact matches to the
    consultation, else the closest. The program the customer is already
    running is left out.
  - Tapping a suggestion opens the program: All Access members get "Start
    program" (one tap onto their calendar); everyone else gets "Unlock with
    All Access", which opens the paywall. So the consultation leads straight
    into the subscription.

Where it buys
  - Website: Stripe subscriptions through our own checkout (Stripe is
    already live). Upgrading Plus -> All Access changes the existing
    subscription (charged pro rata), never a second one. Card changes,
    invoices and cancelling: Stripe's customer portal from Profile.
  - iPhone app 1.0.4+: Apple In-App Purchase through RevenueCat, with
    Apple's own prices. Restore purchases on the paywall. Inside the app
    there's never a website price, link or mention (3.1.1).
  - iPhone app before 1.0.4: the paywall says "Update the APEX app to
    subscribe".
  - Both stores feed one entitlement per customer (soloEntitlements/{uid}),
    so a subscription bought in either works in both.

STEP 3 — CATALOGUE: LAUNCH WITH 5, THEN 10 SOLID PROGRAMS
--------------------------------------------------------
Launch with 5 published programs (owner decision 2026-10-28), then reach
10 solid programs. All Access is only worth A$179.99/year if the catalogue
is deep. Programs are published in the coach console's Train Solo Program
Builder (src/coach/CoachTrainSolo.jsx).

What "solid" means for each program
  - Full length (every week and day filled in), exercises with demo
    videos, sets/reps/rest/RIR set, a progression plan across the weeks.
  - Tagged with goals / style / environment / level / sessions a week so the
    consultation recommends it (src/solo/recommendations.js).
  - Cover artwork uploaded, a clear description, and the owner has run
    through at least the first week in the app.

The 5 for launch (cover the most common consultation answers)
  1. Beginner full-body, gym, 3 days.
  2. Hypertrophy upper/lower, gym, 4 days.
  3. Home / minimal equipment, 3 days.
  4. Fat loss / conditioning, gym, 4 days.
  5. Women's glute and lower-body focus, 3-4 days.
Then 6-10: push/pull/legs, powerbuilding, athletic/performance, 30-40 minute
sessions, bodyweight/travel.

Pace: about one new program every 2-3 weeks after launch until there are 10.


STEP 4 — KEEP CUSTOMERS ENGAGED (ongoing, after launch)
------------------------------------------------------
Subscriptions are won at sign-up and kept by giving people a reason to come
back every week. One visible improvement or new program every 2-4 weeks,
announced with a push and a social post. Web-only items reach every app
version on deploy; "native" ones need an App Store build.

  Month 1 after launch
    - Weekly recap push every Sunday: sessions done, PRs, streak, next
      week's plan.
    - Program finished: a summary (volume, PRs, weeks completed) and the
      consultation's next suggested program, one tap to start (All Access).
    - "Run it again": restart a finished program at heavier targets (needs
      a second run's own log key — see step 2 known limits).
  Month 2
    - Monthly Train Solo challenge (e.g. 12 sessions in 30 days) with a
      badge; leaderboard optional.
    - Trial-ending reminder (2 days before) and a yearly-plan offer for
      monthly subscribers after their second month.
  Month 3
    - Shareable milestone cards (PRs, streaks, program complete) for
      Instagram — free marketing.
    - Win-back: lapsed subscribers get a one-off discount (Stripe coupon;
      Apple win-back offer).
  Month 4+
    - Deload weeks and auto-adjusting targets from RIR.
    - Coaching upsell: All Access members 8+ weeks in see "Want a coach?" —
      the consultation flow into one-to-one coaching.
    - Native (a later build): home-screen widget (today's session, streak),
      Apple Watch rest timer.

  Every new program is an "added to All Access" moment: push to all Train
  Solo users; Free and Plus see it in the catalogue with "Unlock with All
  Access".

  Numbers to watch monthly: trial -> paid conversion, monthly churn, share of
  yearly plans, active customers per week, programs started and finished.
  These decide what to build next.
