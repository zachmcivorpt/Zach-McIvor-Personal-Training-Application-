STEP 2 — MONETISATION: TRAIN SOLO PRO (iOS 1.0.4)
-------------------------------------------------
Goal: earn from Train Solo the way Alpha Progression does — a free app with
a paid Pro subscription bought inside the app, plus the website.

Today: Train Solo sells single programs on the website only (Stripe, live).
The iPhone app hides all buying (Apple 3.1.1, src/solo/appShell.js), so
nobody who installs the app can pay from it.

Coached clients are NOT part of this. Coaching is a one-to-one service
with a real person, which Apple lets you charge for outside the app
(3.1.3(d)); coaching billing stays exactly as it is.

How it works
  - One "Pro" entitlement, the same everywhere: bought in the iPhone app
    through Apple, or on the website through Stripe, it unlocks Pro on both.
  - RevenueCat sits in the middle (free until ~US$2.5k/month revenue, then
    1%). It talks to Apple and Stripe, handles renewals, refunds, trials,
    restore purchases and cancellations, and tells our server who is Pro.
  - Apple keeps 15% on the Small Business Program (free to join, under
    US$1M/year). AU prices include GST, so A$14.99 nets about A$11.58.

Suggested prices (AUD, Apple price points) — owner to decide
  - Monthly A$14.99
  - Yearly  A$99.99 (about 45% off), 7-day free trial
  - Weekly: not recommended. A$8.95/week is ~A$465/year; weekly plans
    drive cancellations and bad reviews.

Suggested Free / Pro split — owner to decide
  Free:  account, workout logging, exercise library and swap, calendar,
         weigh-ins and progress photos, 1 saved custom session, and the
         first week of any program (a taster).
  Pro:   every program in the catalogue, unlimited custom sessions,
         set-by-set progression targets, Apple Health on Progress,
         the recovery add-on, full stats/history.
  Existing buyers keep every program they've already bought, forever.

What gets built
  Web (reaches all app versions on deploy):
    - SoloAppContext: isPro from soloEntitlements/{uid} (server-written
      only; Firestore rules: owner can read, nobody can write).
    - Paywall sheet in Solo glass style: monthly/yearly, trial wording,
      price per period, Restore purchases, Terms of Use and Privacy links,
      "Manage subscription".
    - Pro gates on the features above, each with a tap-to-upgrade path.
    - Website: Pro checkout through RevenueCat Web Billing (Stripe account
      already live) next to the existing program checkout.
    - In the iPhone app the paywall uses Apple only — never a link or
      mention of the website price (3.1.1 still applies).
  Server (Cloud Functions):
    - soloRevenueCatWebhook: verifies the RevenueCat signature and writes
      soloEntitlements/{uid} {pro, expiresAt, store, productId}.
    - Account deletion also clears the entitlement (subscription itself is
      cancelled by the customer in Apple/Stripe; we show how).
  Native (needs the 1.0.4 build + App Review):
    - RevenueCat iOS SDK (Swift Package) and an "apexPurchases" bridge:
      offerings, purchase, restore, customer info. App user ID = Firebase uid.
    - Web shows the paywall only when the bridge exists, so older builds
      never offer a purchase they can't make.
  Tests and register: entitlement rules (emulator), gating, paywall
  wording, webhook verification, old-build hiding — per REGRESSION_TESTS.md.

Owner setup (before building the native part)
  1. App Store Connect > Business: sign the Paid Apps Agreement; add bank
     account and tax forms. Enrol in the App Store Small Business Program.
  2. App Store Connect > the app > Subscriptions: group "Train Solo Pro",
     products monthly + yearly at the chosen prices, 7-day free trial on
     yearly (introductory offer), localised names/descriptions.
  3. App Store Connect > Users and Access > Integrations: an In-App
     Purchase key (.p8) for RevenueCat.
  4. RevenueCat account: add the iOS app and the Stripe account; entitlement
     "pro"; send me the public API keys (iOS + Web) and the webhook secret.
  5. App Store Connect > App Information: App Store Server Notifications V2
     URL = the one RevenueCat gives you.
  6. App Store description: add Terms of Use (Apple's standard EULA is fine)
     and subscription terms — Apple rejects subscription apps without them.

are free), then one App Review as 1.0.4.
