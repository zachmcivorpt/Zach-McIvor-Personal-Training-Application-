Owner Checklist

What to check once the app is live: daily, weekly, monthly, and what to do when something goes wrong.

Daily 2 MINUTES

  • Email: anything from Apple (App Store Connect), Stripe or RevenueCat: reviews, refunds, disputes, billing problems, contract notices.
  • Member emails: reply within a day.
  • App reviews App Store Connect → Ratings and Reviews: reply to any 1–3 star review that mentions a problem.

Weekly 15 MINUTES

  • RevenueCat dashboard: iPhone revenue, active subscribers, trials, cancellations.
  • Stripe dashboard: website sales, refunds, failed payments and disputes (answer before Stripe's deadline).
  • Coach console → Train Solo: new members; paying members show the right plan.
  • App Store Connect → In-App Purchases / Subscriptions: everything still "Approved"; nothing "Developer Action Needed".
  • TestFlight / Codemagic: if a new build was expected, check it arrived.

Monthly

  • Apple payment App Store Connect → Payments and Financial Reports: paid ~33 days after month end. Save the report.
  • Stripe payouts: match to your bank. Save the monthly report.
  • Tax / BAS: give both reports to your accountant.
  • Agreements App Store Connect → Business: Paid Apps Agreement active; bank and tax details current.
  • Prices: website price (coach console) matches the iPhone price (App Store Connect) for every program.
